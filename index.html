family-automation

family-automation is a set of personal, non-commercial home projects run by a single household for itself. They run on a computer in the home and are used only by members of that household. They are not offered to anyone else, and there is nothing to sign up for.

Nest thermostats

Controls the household's Nest thermostats so the house stays comfortable while using less energy. It adjusts setpoints based on the weather forecast, whether the household is home, and comfort feedback from the people who live there.

Backups to Google Drive

Uploads encrypted backups of the thermostat project's data to the household's own Google Drive, so it can be restored if the home computer fails.

Gmail cleanup

Keeps the household's own Gmail inbox tidy by applying rules the household writes: it labels, archives, marks as read, or moves to Trash matching messages, such as old promotions and shipping notices.
